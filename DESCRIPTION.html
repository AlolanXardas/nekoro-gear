Crystals, metals, tools, armor and building blocks in 40 colours.
The base of my other mods, which give these materials more uses.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works with any mods.

Required on the client and the server.

Features

By default the tools, weapons and armor of the new crystals are as strong as netherite, which may be too strong for your modpack. It is better to adjust them in config/Nekoro/Gear/items.json.

Materials

  • 40 materials in the colours of the coins of Nekoro Coins, from red to pink, then white and black. Every Pokémon type has a material in its colour.
  • 24 new crystals: crystal, shard (9 shards make a crystal), block and ores. The ores give shards.
  • 5 new metals (tin, lead, silver, cobalt, mithril): raw metal, nugget, ingot, block, raw block and ores.
  • 2 alloys without ores: 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot make 4 bronze ingots, 3 copper ingots and 1 gold ingot make 4 tumbaga ingots.
  • The other 9 colours are vanilla materials: redstone, copper, gold, emerald, diamond, lapis, amethyst, iron and netherite.
  • Copper gets a nugget, and 9 amethyst shards make a whole amethyst crystal.

Ores

  • Every ore spreads like a vanilla ore: the most common materials like copper, most like iron, the rarest like diamonds.
  • Crystal ores are single blocks and half as common as their vanilla ore. Metal ores come in veins like vanilla.
  • In the stone layer (Y 0 and up) ores are half as common as in the deepslate layer. The config sets the amount for each layer, or turns ores off.
  • Stone and deepslate versions, Fortune and Silk Touch work like on vanilla ores.
  • With the ores turned off, the materials can come from Nekoro Random Drop instead.

Tools and armor

  • Sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots for every material except iron, gold, diamond and netherite, which already have them.
  • Metals, alloys and vanilla materials sit near a vanilla tier, from below gold (redstone, lapis) to diamond.
  • Durability, damage, speed, mining tier, enchantability, defense, toughness and knockback resistance can be set for every single item in the config.
  • Crafted like vanilla tools and armor, and repaired with their material.
  • Metal tools and armor smelt back into nuggets.
  • The armor takes armor trims.

Building blocks

  • For all 40 materials: large bricks, small bricks and a smooth block, each with stairs and a slab, plus a pillar, a lamp and a redstone lamp.
  • Bricks and smooth blocks: 8 stone bricks or smooth stone around one piece of the material make 8 blocks. The piece is a shard for crystals, an ingot for metals, or the vanilla item. Lamps use the same piece.
  • Only the plain storage block takes 9 whole crystals or ingots and can be turned back into them.
  • Stairs, slabs, small bricks and pillars can also be made in a stonecutter.
  • No recipe turns the blocks back into the material.

With my other mods

  • Nekoro Random Drop: mined stone and deepslate drop metal nuggets about as often as vanilla ores of the same rarity give their ore. Crystal shards drop only from deepslate below Y 0, twice as often as netherite scrap and with a diamond pickaxe or better. Fortune works, and a pickaxe of a material doubles the chance of that material. Good for worlds with the ores of this mod turned off.
  • Nekoro Coins: the coins have the same colours and names, and its config has recipes for them from these materials.
  • Nekoro Collector Edition: the bags are crafted from the material of their colour.
  • More uses will come with my next mods.

More information

Config

  • All files are in config/Nekoro/Gear/ and every option is explained in the file. Changes take effect after a restart.
  • options.json: how many ores generate in the deepslate layer and in the stone layer, from none to more than vanilla.
  • items.json: the stats of every tool, weapon and armor piece.

Item tags

  • Crystals, ingots, nuggets, raw metals, ores and storage blocks have the common tags, for example #c:gems/ruby, #c:ingots/tin and #c:ores/sapphire, so recipes of other mods can use them.
  • Tools and armor are in the vanilla tags, so enchantments work on them.
